About Me

Welcome! I'm Annika Armstrong. Over my 8+ years of teaching and tutoring, I have gained a lifelong passion for helping students take ownership of their learning. For three years, I taught high school English full-time, designing curriculum for grades 9-12 and AP English Language & Composition. During that time, I guided my AP students in improving exam pass rates from 28% to 88% over two years. After my students developed literacy skills that fostered their sense of self-efficacy beyond the classroom, I knew I had found my purpose. I am currently completing my Master's in Literature at the University of Colorado Boulder, where I continue to improve my own teaching practice while also supporting graduate instructors.

Why Choose Me?

  • I help students build confidence and independence, not just improve their grades.
  • I offer flexible scheduling and send weekly updates to parents.
  • My breadth of experience allows me to adapt sessions to fit each student's needs.
  • I balance support with high expectations so students feel encouraged and capable.
  • I help students with skills that will last beyond their current class, such as critical thinking, study techniques, and time management.
